Demonstration — tender matching

We pointed the matching engine at one real company and asked: what should it bid?

The engine reduced 653 open notices to 167 sector candidates, 45 in the company's home market — and the next layer separates direct bids, partner-assembly opportunities, and no-gos. No shortlist was prepared by hand; one pass on 2026-08-09.

Minimum threshold to appear on this page: 35. A category match alone contributes 45 — which clears it. We show the arithmetic rather than dress it up.

The fit-score measures relevance, not win-probability. Route separation (§04) and spec-level analysis are the layers above — a high score says the engine found a sector match, not that the bid will succeed.

This scoring layer is deliberately simple. The hard assets sit beneath it — a multi-market tender registry with a time-series corpus that public sources no longer serve — and above it: spec-level document analysis, compliance reasoning and per-tender execution assembly, the layers that produced a winning HK$29,962,990 government bid. The arithmetic is published because the moat was never the arithmetic.

Top-20 human review: 4 direct-path · 9 partner-assembly · 7 not routable. The engine generates candidates; the honest number is the point — it makes the system measurable.
