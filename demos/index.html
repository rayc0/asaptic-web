§05

Gate record · 双闸门

Two gates killed every “looks-matched-but-can’t-deliver” path.

A match is not a bid. Every candidate route ran both gates; routes that passed the first but failed the second were discarded — because a bid you win and cannot deliver is worse than no bid.

投标门 · Bid gate

  • 资格 · qualification
  • 中标概率 · win-probability
  • 方案可签署 · a signable assembled solution

履约门 · Delivery gate

  • 真实货源 · real goods
  • 许可证 + 跨境运输 · licences & transport
  • 验收 + 现金流 + 担责 · acceptance, cash-flow, responsibility

投标门通过 × 履约门不过 → 剔除 DISCARDED — 我们从不把「能匹配」当成「能中标」。

What the two gates excluded
投标门通过、履约门不过的路径被剔除 — 例如货源不落地、跨境运输不可控、验收或担责结构不清。只有两道门都过，才组装进最终方案。
