We didn't just win a HK$29,962,990 government contract. We won the part no catalog search could.

This is a reconstruction of the actual awarded bid (GLD Contract C0009/2026) — the hard decisions, traced step by step, each expandable to the real evidence. §02

The ask

A government department wanted a "mock plane." That is not a product you can buy.

The requirement was a full-scale mock aircraft — for a Hong Kong Government training facility. There is no catalog line, no supplier list, no reference price. A keyword search returns nothing biddable; a matching engine scores it "other." The difficulty is not writing the bid — it is turning a use-case ("a realistic mock plane") into a concrete, procurable, deliverable bill of materials, then finding who on earth can supply it.

▸ 展开：为什么普通检索会失败

目录检索和关键词匹配假设"需求=某个已存在的商品"。"模拟飞机"没有商品条目、没有供应商名录、没有参考价——它是一次性组装件，必须先被拆解成真实航材，才谈得上采购。

§03

Step 01 · Read

The AI cracked "mock plane" into real, procurable aircraft parts.

The engine resolved the requirement into a concrete structure: retired Airbus A330-200 fuselage sections plus Boeing 777-200 doors, assembled with supporting structure into a full mock airframe — approximately 85 tonnes as modified per the technical specification, including supporting columns. This is the step that separates a real bid from a plausible one: not "we'll supply a mock plane," but the exact retired-airframe bill of materials that satisfies the spec.

§04

Step 02 · Source

Then it found supply where no ordinary bidder would look.

Retired wide-body fuselage sections are not a market commodity. The engine located supply inside the 宜昌 aircraft-dismantling industrial chain — the ecosystem where retired airframes are broken down — a source you reach only if you already understand where decommissioned aircraft physically go. This is the moat in one move: not the cleverness of any single search, but the accumulated map of where non-catalog goods actually live.

▸ 展开：为什么这一步是壁垒

退役宽体机身段不在任何供应商目录里；它们藏在飞机拆解产业链的物理流向中。找到它需要的不是通用搜索能力，而是一张随每单积累、别人无法追回的"非目录货源地图"。

§05

Step 03 · Two gates

Two gates killed every "looks-matched-but-can't-deliver" path.

A match is not a bid. Every candidate route ran two gates. 投标门 (Bid gate): qualification, win-probability, and whether the assembled solution is signable. 履约门 (Delivery gate): real goods, licences, cross-border transport, acceptance, cash-flow and who carries responsibility. Routes that passed the bid gate but failed the delivery gate were discarded — because a bid you win but cannot deliver is worse than no bid.

▸ 展开：双闸门排除了什么

投标门通过、履约门不过的路径被剔除——例如货源不落地、跨境运输不可控、验收或担责结构不清。只有两道门都过，才组装进最终方案。
