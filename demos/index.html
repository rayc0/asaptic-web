Reconstructed case · how the bid was won

We didn’t just win a HK$29,962,990 government contract. We won the part no catalog search could.

A step-by-step reconstruction of the actual awarded bid — GLD Contract C0009/2026 — with the real evidence behind each decision. The point isn’t that an AI produced a document. It’s that the AI found what an ordinary bidder could not, and every step deposited into an asset that compounds.

1

The ask

A government department wanted a “mock plane.” That is not a product you can buy.

The requirement was a full-scale mock aircraft for a Hong Kong Government training facility. There is no catalog line, no supplier list, no reference price. A keyword search returns nothing biddable; a matching engine scores it “other.” The difficulty was never writing the bid — it was turning a use-case into a concrete, procurable, deliverable bill of materials, and then finding who on earth could supply it.

Why ordinary retrieval fails here
目录检索和关键词匹配都假设「需求 = 某个已存在的商品」。「模拟飞机」没有商品条目、没有供应商名录、没有参考价 — 它是一次性组装件，必须先被拆解成真实航材，才谈得上采购。这一层，通用大模型加几天工程做不出来。

Step 01 · Read

The engine cracked “mock plane” into real, procurable aircraft parts.

It resolved the requirement into a concrete structure: retired Airbus A330-200 fuselage sections plus Boeing 777-200 doors, assembled with supporting structure into a full mock airframe — approximately 85 tonnes as modified, including supporting columns, per the technical specification. This is the step that separates a real bid from a plausible one: not “we’ll supply a mock plane,” but the exact retired-airframe bill of materials that satisfies the spec.

Step 02 · Source

Then it found supply where no ordinary bidder would look.

Retired wide-body fuselage sections are not a market commodity. The engine located supply inside the 宜昌 aircraft-dismantling industrial chain — the ecosystem where decommissioned airframes are physically broken down — a source you reach only if you already understand where retired aircraft actually go. This is the moat in a single move: not the cleverness of any one search, but the accumulated map of where non-catalog goods really live.

Why this step is the barrier
退役宽体机身段不在任何供应商目录里；它们藏在飞机拆解产业链的物理流向中。找到它需要的不是通用搜索能力，而是一张随每一单积累、后来者无法追回的「非目录货源地图」。
4

Step 03 · Two gates

Two gates killed every “looks-matched-but-can’t-deliver” path.

A match is not a bid. Every candidate route ran two gates. The bid gate — qualification, win-probability, and whether the assembled solution is actually signable. The delivery gate — real goods, licences, cross-border transport, acceptance, cash-flow, and who carries responsibility. Routes that passed the first but failed the second were discarded — because a bid you win and cannot deliver is worse than no bid.

What the two gates excluded
投标门通过、履约门不过的路径被剔除 — 例如货源不落地、跨境运输不可控、验收或担责结构不清。只有两道门都过，才组装进最终方案。这也是为什么我们从不把「能匹配」当成「能中标」。

Why it repeats

The win wasn’t the point. What each win deposits is.
