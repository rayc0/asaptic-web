China export standards for Cosmetics & personal care

Cosmetics and personal care products exported from China—covering skincare, colour cosmetics, hair care, and oral hygiene products—are regulated through ingredient-restriction lists, safety assessment obligations, product notification or registration requirements, and labelling mandates that differ significantly between markets. The EU Cosmetics Regulation maintains a list of over 1,300 restricted or prohibited substances and requires a Cosmetic Product Safety Report and CPNP notification; the US under MoCRA now requires facility registration and product listing; other markets maintain their own positive or negative ingredient lists. Claims substantiation, preservative systems, and microbiological specifications must each be documented.
