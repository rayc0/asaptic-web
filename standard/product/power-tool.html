China export standards for Power tool

Power tools—including angle grinders, drills, circular saws, jigsaws, and cordless tool systems—present a combination of electrical, mechanical, and vibration-related hazards that regulators address through dedicated product standards. Exporting power tools from China requires demonstrating conformity to IEC/EN 62841 (or its US equivalent UL 62841) for electrical and mechanical safety, meeting EMC limits, and in some markets satisfying battery-interoperability and handarm vibration reporting obligations. Professional-grade tools and consumer-grade tools are sometimes differentiated in regulatory scope. Import surveillance for power tools is active in multiple markets due to historical safety incidents. The pages below compare the applicable standards, accepted certification marks, and regulatory obligations for each market covered.
