China export standards for Furniture

Furniture exported from China spans a wide range of product types—upholstered seating, mattresses, case goods, office furniture, and outdoor furniture—each subject to overlapping safety, chemical, and sustainability requirements. Flammability standards are particularly divergent: California's TB 117-2013 and TB 133, the UK's Furniture and Furnishings Fire Safety Regulations, and the EU's forthcoming updated requirements impose different fire-resistance benchmarks and testing methodologies. Formaldehyde emission limits for composite wood products (CARB Phase 2 in California; EU E1 class) require third-party board certification. REACH restrictions apply to surface coatings, fillers, and fasteners. Structural and mechanical durability tests vary by product type and end use.
