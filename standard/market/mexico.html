China export standards for Mexico (NOM / COFEPRIS)

Mexico's mandatory product standards are published as Normas Oficiales Mexicanas (NOM) by the relevant Secretariat — primarily the Secretariat of Economy (SE) for industrial and commercial products, and the Secretariat of Energy (SENER) for energy-related equipment. COFEPRIS (Federal Commission for Protection against Sanitary Risk) regulates medical devices, pharmaceuticals, and food-contact materials. Products subject to NOM must be certified by an accredited certification body (Organismo de Certificación) and may require NOM labelling. Exporters from China should verify which NOM standards apply to their specific products and engage a Mexican importer or representative.
