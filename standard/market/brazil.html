China export standards for Brazil (INMETRO)

Brazil's national metrology and conformity body, INMETRO (Instituto Nacional de Metrologia, Qualidade e Tecnologia), oversees mandatory product certification across a wide range of categories. Many regulated products require certification through an INMETRO-accredited body before import or sale, and must display the applicable conformity mark. Health surveillance authority ANVISA governs medical devices, pharmaceuticals, and food-contact materials. The Brazilian technical standards body, ABNT, publishes the NBR standards that often align with international IEC or ISO references but may contain Brazil-specific provisions. Exporters from China must engage locally accredited laboratories and Brazilian importers to navigate the mandatory approval processes.
