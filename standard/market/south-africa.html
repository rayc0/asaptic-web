China export standards for South Africa (SABS / NERSA)

South Africa's standards and conformity ecosystem is anchored by the South African Bureau of Standards (SABS), which publishes SANS (South African National Standards) — many of which are aligned with IEC or ISO references. The National Regulator for Compulsory Specifications (NRCS) administers mandatory technical regulations (compulsory specifications, VC numbers) for a range of regulated products including electrical equipment. For energy infrastructure, the National Energy Regulator of South Africa (NERSA) has oversight of grid-connected generation and storage. Exporters from China must verify whether a product is subject to a compulsory specification and engage an NRCS-recognised body for approval.
