Open Dataset · asaptic.tender.v1

The weekly public-sector tender dataset, machine-readable.

Every active tender opportunity Asaptic publishes each week — across seven markets — as open JSON, Atom and RSS. Built to be read by agents and pipelines, not just people. Category, trilingual summary, rough value band and closing window per listing. No registration to consume.

Schema — asaptic.tender.v1

Eleven fields per listing. Deadlines are expressed only as a closing window bucket, never an exact date. Feeds carry active opportunities only.

FieldDescription
asaptic_idStable opportunity id, e.g. AT-TEST-0001-001.
marketOne of HK, SG, MO, GB, AU, CA, EU.
categoryCategory name in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
summary_en / summary_zh / summary_zhtOne-line summary in each language.
value_bandlt_500k · 500k_2m · 2m_10m · gt_10m · null.
closing_bucketle_2w · 2_4w · gt_4w · deadline_passed.
lead_okWhether a supplier specification pack can be requested for this listing.
new_this_issueWhether the listing is new in the current weekly issue.
sort_keyOpaque stable ordering key.

Terms

The dataset is free to browse and consume under the Asaptic terms of use. Listings are sanitized: each row is a category-level opportunity summary. To request the full supplier specification pack for a listing, register free at portal.asaptic.com.
