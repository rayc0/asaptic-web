Security

Responsible disclosure

Asaptic (HK) Ltd welcomes reports of security issues in our public surfaces. If you have found a vulnerability, tell us — we commit to reading every good-faith report, acting on it, and never pursuing researchers who follow this policy.

How to report

Contactengage@asaptic.com
Machine-readable/.well-known/security.txt
LanguagesEnglish · 简体中文 · 繁體中文
StandardRFC 9116

Email engage@asaptic.com with a subject line beginning [SECURITY]. Include the affected URL, a description of the issue, and the steps to reproduce it. If a proof of concept is useful, keep it minimal and non-destructive.

In scope

asaptic.com — the public site /tender/rows.json — public feed /mcp — sourcing MCP server /.well-known/*

Out of scope

— volumetric or denial-of-service testing — social engineering of staff or partners — physical access attempts — reports from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact

Our commitment

  • We will acknowledge a good-faith report and keep you updated as we investigate.
  • We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service degradation, and give us reasonable time to remediate before public discussion.
  • We will credit reporters who wish to be named once an issue is resolved.

Please give us a reasonable window to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly. Access only data that is your own or plainly test data, and stop at the first sign that a test could affect other users.

Verify our surfaces

Asaptic publishes a falsifiable proof manifest and a live status page. Anyone can check what we claim against what the endpoints actually serve:
