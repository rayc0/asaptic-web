Frequently asked questions
What is included in the free weekly tender listing?
The complete sanitized list of Hong Kong tenders published this week — category, summary, rough value band, and closing window per row — sourced from government departments and public bodies & institutions. No registration required to browse the listings.
Do I need to register to see the tender listings?
No. The full sanitized weekly listing is free and public. Registration at portal.asaptic.com is only needed to request the full supplier specification pack for a specific tender.
Who is this tender digest for?
Suppliers in mainland China and Hong Kong who want to bid into Hong Kong public-sector procurement — covering both government departments and public bodies & institutions.
How often is the list updated?
Weekly, covering newly published tenders and their closing deadlines for that week.
Listings are summarized from publicly available Hong Kong government and public-body tender sources. Asaptic is not the procuring body for any tender referenced here and is not affiliated with the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.
