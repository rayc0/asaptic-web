Frequently asked questions

What is included in the free weekly tender count?
The total number of open Hong Kong tenders this week, broken down by category, plus their closing dates — sourced from government departments and public bodies & institutions. No registration required to view the counts.
Do I need to register to see the actual tender list?
Yes. The counts are free and public; the full sanitized list of individual tenders (with product/service category, region, and closing date, but no source-department identifiers beyond the general classification) requires a one-time free registration at portal.asaptic.com.
Who is this tender digest for?
Suppliers in mainland China and Hong Kong who want to bid into Hong Kong public-sector procurement — covering both government departments and public bodies & institutions.
How often is the list updated?
Weekly, covering newly opened tenders and their closing deadlines for that week.
Listings are summarized from publicly available Hong Kong government and public-body tender sources. Asaptic is not the procuring body for any tender referenced here and is not affiliated with the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.
