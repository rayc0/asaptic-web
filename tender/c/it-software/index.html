Frequently asked questions
What IT and software work does Hong Kong's public sector tender?
Systems integration, application development and licensing, cloud and hosting, cybersecurity, network infrastructure, and IT support — across government departments and public bodies.
Why does this category have more tenders than others?
Continuous digitalisation initiatives across the public sector generate a steady, high-volume flow of both large systems contracts and smaller service tenders every week.
