Hong Kong Construction & Works Tenders

Hong Kong construction & works tenders this week: — open, soonest closing —. Government departments and public bodies buy into this category on a recurring basis. Asaptic tracks these weekly — register free for the full sanitized list.

Government departments and public bodies procure construction, renovation, maintenance, and engineering works for public facilities and infrastructure — ranging from minor building works to larger civil engineering projects.

Tender scale varies widely: minor works and maintenance contracts open frequently, while larger infrastructure projects appear less often but run over longer terms. Site visits, technical method statements, and programme submissions are commonly required as part of the bid.

Suppliers typically need relevant contractor registration or gazetting status for the works category, safety and quality certifications, adequate insurance and bonding capacity for the contract value, and demonstrated project experience at a comparable scale.
