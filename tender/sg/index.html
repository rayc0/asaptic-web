Frequently asked questions
What kinds of tenders are covered?
The digest includes goods, services and works tenders from Singapore public-sector entities such as government ministries, statutory boards, town councils and universities.
Is access to the full weekly digest free?
Yes. Supplier registration is free, and registered users can view the full sanitised list of opportunities.
Does the digest replace official tender sources?
No. The digest summarises publicly available information for commercial reference; always verify details against the original tender documents before bidding.
Listings are summarized from publicly available Singapore public-sector procurement publications (GeBIZ, statutory boards, town councils, universities and other public bodies). Asaptic is not the procuring body for any tender referenced here and is not affiliated with the Government of Singapore.
